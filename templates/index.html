About Us


Smart Blood System is a simple and user-friendly platform that connects blood donors with people who need blood. Donors can register by providing their basic details and blood group, while receivers can search for suitable donors and submit blood requests. The platform helps users quickly find matching blood groups and makes the process of connecting donors and receivers easier. Use the Register option to create an account, Login to access your dashboard, and Search Donors to find available blood donors. Always verify donor and receiver details through the appropriate medical or hospital authority before arranging a donation.
